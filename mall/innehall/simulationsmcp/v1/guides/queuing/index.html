📘

Simple Queue (M/M/1) →

A single-server queuing system where items arrive randomly, wait in a queue, are processed by one server, and depart. This is the foundational discrete-event pattern for any service or processing system.

📘

Multi-Server Queue (M/M/c) →

A queuing system with multiple parallel servers sharing a single waiting line. Items wait in one queue and are routed to the next available server, modeling call centers, bank teller lines, or parallel machines.

📘

Priority Queue →

A queuing system where items are served in priority order rather than arrival order. High-priority items jump ahead of lower-priority items in the queue, modeling emergency services, VIP lanes, or job scheduling with urgency levels.
