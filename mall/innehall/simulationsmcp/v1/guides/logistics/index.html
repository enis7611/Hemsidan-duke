Multi-Stage Supply Chain →

A sequential supply chain model with warehouse, transportation, and retail stages. Each stage has its own processing time and queue, representing the flow of goods from supplier through distribution to end customer.

📘

Inventory Management (Reorder Point) →

A reorder-point inventory model where a Gate block checks stock level and either fulfills demand immediately from stock or places a replenishment order. Models the classic (s, S) or (R, Q) inventory policy.
